PRIVACY NOTICE

Privacy at RunnerBear

RunnerBear is a personal running-coach web app. It is designed to keep training and health data private, local and limited to what is needed for coaching decisions.

What RunnerBear processes

RunnerBear may process training and recovery information made available by Tredict, including completed activities, distance, duration, pace, heart rate, HRV, sleep and resting-heart-rate related values. RunnerBear may also store user-entered coaching data such as perceived exertion, Achilles response, goals and training preferences.

Training dataCompleted activities and workout metrics used to reconcile planned and completed training.
Recovery dataHRV, sleep and resting-heart-rate related values used as conservative recovery signals.
Coach dataGoals, training blocks, feedback, control sessions and training history.
Local app dataApp preferences and cached data required for RunnerBear to function on the device.

How Tredict access is used

RunnerBear requests read access only for the Tredict data needed to display completed activities and recovery information. The intended initial scopes are activityRead and bodyvaluesRead.

RunnerBear does not require write access to Tredict for the initial integration. Good recovery data is not used to add bonus training; it normally means the planned session remains unchanged.

Where data is stored

RunnerBear is currently a static web app hosted on GitHub Pages. Personal training and health data are not committed to the public GitHub repository. App data and cached Tredict data are stored locally in the user’s browser on the device unless a future sync feature is explicitly enabled.

Authentication credentials or access tokens must never be stored in the public repository. RunnerBear backup files are designed not to include Tredict authentication tokens.

Sharing and sale of data

RunnerBear does not sell personal data, use health data for advertising, or intentionally share training or health data with third parties. Data requested from Tredict is used only to provide RunnerBear coaching, recovery and training-history functionality for the user.

Retention and deletion

Locally stored RunnerBear data remains on the device until the user removes it, clears browser storage, restores a backup, or uses RunnerBear’s reset functionality. Tredict access can be disconnected by removing the connection in RunnerBear and/or revoking authorization in Tredict.

Security

RunnerBear follows data-minimisation principles: request the smallest practical set of permissions, prefer read-only access, keep credentials out of source control and avoid collecting data that is not needed for coaching decisions.

Contact

RunnerBear is currently a personal, non-commercial project. Questions about this privacy notice or the use of Tredict data can be directed to the RunnerBear project owner through the project’s GitHub account.
